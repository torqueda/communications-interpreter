Letter Explainer (working title)

Upload an official English letter and get it back in plain Spanish: a side-by-side translation with the important sentences color-matched across both languages, plus a short summary of what the letter is, what it asks, by when, and who to contact.

The unique kernel

The family doesn't have to know how to prompt, what to ask, or how to check the answer. Every point is traced back to the exact sentence it came from.

A purpose-built workflow extracts the important information, color-codes each point in both the original and the translation, runs a verifier agent over the output, and flags letters that look like scams. It's a free tool, not a product: "if you need help with this specific scenario, here's a tool you or your family can use."

How it flows

What "working" looks like

A made-up, confusing, Medicaid-style notice goes in. Within a minute, the screen shows the color-matched side-by-side and a short summary. The memorable moment comes when a deadline highlighted in the Spanish text matches the same color in the English original, so anyone can check it. Hover or tap a color below.

Who it's for

First-generation Hispanic immigrant adults who struggle with English, and the bilingual family members who help them. Example: a parent gets a Medicaid notice in their online portal and needs to know whether it's urgent and what to do. Today they guess, wait for a relative, or paste it into a chatbot without knowing whether to trust the answer.

The core loop

Open the app, then drop in or browse for a letter. A friendly animation plays while it works. On the same screen they get the side-by-side, the summary, and a warning if it looks like a scam. They come back whenever another letter arrives.

Trial letters

This time the plan is to "run more trial cases, contemplating more potential scenarios for right and wrong outcomes."

  • More found during planning

What's in, what waits, what's cut

Now: the proof of concept

9 items
  • Single-file upload: one PDF or one image
  • Side-by-side original and Spanish translation, key sentences color-matched (no line-by-line alignment needed)
  • Structured summary: what it is, what it asks, deadline, required documents, who to contact
  • Verifier agent that checks the translation and extraction
  • Scam/phishing flag
  • English/Spanish interface toggle
  • Disclosures section with a "not legal advice" note
  • A set of made-up test letters covering the scenarios above
  • Fabricated demo letters only; no real personal data

Later

5 items
  • Multiple screenshots of one letter, including out-of-order parts (documented so it isn't forgotten)
  • History tabs for past documents and sessions (stores sensitive letters, so needs a privacy design)
  • Save the deadline to a calendar
  • Photos of physical paper letters (a README next step, not stubbed in code)
  • Hardening for real users before recommending it to anyone

Explicitly cut

2 items
  • Code skeleton for physical-letter capture (costs build time, doesn't show in the demo)
  • Monetization (deliberately free; the goal is access)
